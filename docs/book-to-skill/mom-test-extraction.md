# The Mom Test Book-to-Skill Extraction

## Source Summary

- Core principle: customer conversations produce useful truth only when questions avoid leading, opinions, compliments, and future hypotheticals.
- Intended use case: improve sales and discovery conversations by asking about past behavior, concrete facts, current constraints, and real commitments.
- Reusable method: ask about the customer's life, avoid pitching early, deflect compliments, seek commitment or advancement.
- Guardrails: praise is not progress; vague future interest is weak data; do not manipulate customers or disguise a sales pitch as research.

## Asset Mapping

- `SOUL.md`: add a behavior default to avoid leading questions and treat compliments as weak signals.
- `MEMORY.md`: remember Mom Test rules for real-demand discovery before product recommendation.
- `Skill`: `ask-with-mom-test`, a reusable workflow for rewriting questions and judging customer signals.
- `Knowledge Base`: keep the extraction note and reference question banks outside default prompt.

## Draft Outputs

### SOUL.md candidates

- Ask about past behavior before future intent.
- Do not ask questions that reveal the answer sales wants to hear.
- Treat compliments and vague interest as weak signals until the customer gives a concrete next step.

### MEMORY.md candidates

- The Mom Test in this profile means: ask about the customer's life, ask about specifics in the past, and talk less about your own product before the need is clear.
- Praise, generic opinions, and hypothetical buying intent are not reliable demand signals.
- Meaningful progress requires time, reputation, money, visit, sample review, model confirmation, or another concrete next step.

### Skill Created

- Name: `ask-with-mom-test`
- Purpose: help 有膜有漾 sales ask truthful, non-leading C-end car-film questions.
- Trigger: customer need is unclear, sales question sounds biased, or customer gives praise without action.
- Workflow: classify signal, rewrite bad question, ask past-specific questions, seek small commitment, then hand off to SPIN/recommendation/objection skills.
