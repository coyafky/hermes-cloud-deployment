## Core Frameworks Adapted

- Tactical empathy: understand and verbalize the customer's perspective before advancing the sale.
- Mirroring: repeat key words to invite more detail without interrogation.
- Labeling: name the likely emotion or concern to lower defensiveness.
- Accusation audit: address likely distrust before the customer has to defend against sales pressure.
- Calibrated questions: ask "what/how" questions that help customers define constraints and workable next steps.
- "That's right": summarize facts plus emotion until the customer confirms the real issue.
- Black Swan search: uncover hidden constraints such as decision-maker, budget cap, past bad experience, schedule pressure, or distrust.
