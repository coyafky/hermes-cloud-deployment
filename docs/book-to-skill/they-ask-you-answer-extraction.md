# They Ask, You Answer Book-to-Skill Extraction

## Source Summary

- Core principle: buyers trust companies that honestly answer the questions they are already asking, especially uncomfortable questions about price, problems, comparisons, reviews, and best choices.
- Intended use case: turn common buyer questions into transparent sales answers and educational assets.
- Reusable method: The Big 5 plus Assignment Selling.
- Guardrails: transparency is not overpromising; price, policy, inventory, and schedule still require current store confirmation.

## Asset Mapping

- `SOUL.md`: add a behavior default to answer real customer concerns directly and transparently.
- `MEMORY.md`: remember The Big 5 and Assignment Selling as sales education rules.
- `Skill`: `answer-customer-faq-transparently`, a reusable workflow for answering price, risk, comparison, review, and best-fit questions.
- `Knowledge Base`: keep FAQ prompts and templates as references outside the default prompt.

## Draft Outputs

### SOUL.md candidates

- Answer the question the customer is really asking, especially price, risk, comparison, and fit.
- Do not hide uncomfortable tradeoffs; explain factors and boundaries.
- Never invent real-time price, promotions, reviews, inventory, or construction schedule.

### MEMORY.md candidates

- The Big 5 for car film sales are cost, problems, comparisons, reviews, and best-fit choices.
- Assignment Selling means sending helpful education before a quote, store visit, package comparison, or follow-up.

### Skill Created

- Name: `answer-customer-faq-transparently`
- Purpose: help 有膜有漾 sales answer high-trust customer questions transparently.
- Trigger: customer asks about price, risk, comparison, reviews, or best choice.
- Workflow: classify the FAQ, answer directly, explain factors, state fit/non-fit, cite source-backed facts, end with a next step.
